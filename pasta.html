Garlic Pasta

Garlic pasta with herbs and plenty of freshly grated Parmesan cheese. Chop the garlic so that some is minced and some is in larger pieces.

Ingredients

  • (16 ounce) package dry penne pasta
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 1 medium head garlic, peeled and chopped
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh oregano
  • 1 tablespoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  3. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add penne and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. While the pasta is cooking, add oil and garlic to a large skillet over low heat. Cook until oil is hot and garlic is sizzling, 8 to 12 minutes. Remove from the heat and stir in parsley, basil, oregano, and pepper flakes.
  5. Drain the pasta and add it to the skillet with the garlic mixture.
  6. Mix well and top with grated Parmesan cheese.
